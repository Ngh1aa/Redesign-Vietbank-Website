# Vietbank V7 — Visual Signature

## Strongest cues to preserve
- Official Vietbank logo asset.
- Institutional blue as the primary navigation/action/trust color.
- Red + yellow from the mark as controlled secondary accents.
- Existing compact utility navigation and explicit official-channel handoffs.
- Numbered section hierarchy already used across V6.

## Digital signature system

### 1. Dual-accent keyline
A short **red → yellow** line appears inside the existing section-label grammar and selected source/status details.

Rules:
- never larger than the primary blue information hierarchy;
- never used to indicate financial loss/gain, error/success or urgency;
- repeat across page families as a recognition cue, not decoration.

### 2. Ledger numbering
Use tabular, compact numbering for:
- section labels;
- service/top-task rows;
- evidence/source lists;
- process stages where sequence matters.

This extends existing V6 numbering into a more banking-native hierarchy.

### 3. Blue institutional keyline
Thin blue keylines or left rules can denote the active/primary information block. Avoid large saturated-blue cards unless the section truly needs a dominant institutional moment.

### 4. Verified-object imagery
When the object itself is the product (cards), use current first-party Vietbank product imagery instead of generic icons or synthetic mockups.

### 5. Source-forward microcopy
Sensitive/current information visibly exposes its truth boundary:
- `Nguồn Vietbank`
- `Mở kênh chính thức`
- `Điều kiện theo công bố hiện hành`

Trust language is part of the brand experience, not footer-only legal copy.

## Generic category patterns to avoid
- logo + blue + generic rounded-card soup;
- fake fintech dashboard charts;
- glowing gradients/glass effects unrelated to Vietbank;
- fake card mockups with invented numbers;
- every page using the same 3-card composition;
- oversized lifestyle slogans with insufficient decision information.

## Application examples

### Correct
- Home quick tasks as separated ledger rows with numbers and direct outcome labels.
- Card page showing actual Vietbank card art with normal semantic product information below.
- Investor facts using dated editorial blocks with source links.

### Incorrect
- Making a whole homepage resemble a printed bank statement.
- Using paper textures, torn receipt edges or plastic-card 3D tilt as decorative effects.
- Using red/yellow for every button because they exist in the logo.

## Recognition QA

When logo/header/footer are visually removed, representative pages should still share at least three independent cues:
1. blue institutional keyline/action system;
2. red/yellow dual-accent detail;
3. numbered ledger/editorial hierarchy;
4. source-forward trust microcopy where appropriate;
5. real product-object presentation on card surfaces.

This is an internal consistency heuristic, not proof of market-level brand recognition.
