# Audience Intent & Top Tasks — Vietbank V7

> Evidence level: no first-party analytics/search-log/call-centre dataset is available in this repository. Rankings below combine official public product/service evidence with professional hypothesis and must not be called user-research validated.

## 1. Audience groups

### A. Prospective personal customer
- Trigger: needs an everyday account, savings, loan, card or digital banking entry point.
- Likely constraint: mobile entry from search/social/campaign.
- Key risk: product/fee uncertainty and phishing/unofficial links.
- Success: reaches the right verified product and understands the next step.

### B. Existing personal customer
- Trigger: needs a quick utility, Digital Plus, branch, form, fee/rate or urgent support.
- Urgency: medium to high.
- Success: completes self-service or reaches an official Vietbank support channel with minimal detour.

### C. SME / business customer
- Trigger: account operations, working capital, payment/cash management, trade finance or DigiBiz.
- Decision context: may involve owner, finance/accounting and approvers.
- Success: understands solution fit and moves to DigiBiz/forms/advisor with the right context.

### D. Investor/shareholder
- Trigger: VBB disclosure, results, governance or corporate event.
- Success: reaches dated official disclosure, not a fabricated market-data widget.

## 2. Top-task ranking

| Rank | Task | Audience | Evidence/confidence | Website responsibility |
|---:|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Reach the correct digital banking channel | A/B/C | HIGH — Digital Plus/DigiBiz are current first-party products | distinguish and hand off clearly |
| 2 | Find a product by financial need | A/C | MEDIUM — product catalogue + category convention | need-based routing, not org chart |
| 3 | Check rate/fee/form/branch information | B/C | HIGH — official utility pages exist | global utility access |
| 4 | Compare/select a card | A | HIGH — large current Vietbank card catalogue | verified product comparison + real card assets |
| 5 | Get support / verify a suspicious situation | B | HIGH — official hotline/security messaging | visible official-channel guidance |
| 6 | Review investor/corporate evidence | D/partners | MEDIUM | dated source-forward presentation |

## 4. Homepage implications

- First decision: **Cá nhân / Doanh nghiệp**, but do not lock users into a persona permanently.
- Next: top-task ledger with direct task labels.
- Financial utilities appear as a service board, not fake live values.
- Trust statement precedes sensitive handoffs.
- News is secondary to tasks and service continuity.
