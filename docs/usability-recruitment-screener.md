# Vietbank — Sprint 3 Recruitment Screener

## Purpose
Recruit people whose recent behavior can answer the Sprint 3 research questions. Do not reveal the expected navigation or product names in the screener.

## Intro
We are evaluating the usability of a banking-website redesign concept. The session is about the interface, not your financial knowledge. You will not be asked for passwords, OTP, account numbers, transaction IDs or other sensitive banking information.

## Core questions

1. In the last 6 months, which banking activities have you done online? Select all that apply.
   - checked savings/deposit rates;
   - compared cards;
   - looked up fees/forms;
   - used a loan/savings calculator;
   - found a branch/support channel;
   - used a personal banking app;
   - used a business banking platform;
   - none of these.

2. When you need banking information, which device do you most often use?
   - smartphone;
   - laptop/desktop;
   - both about equally;
   - other.

3. How confident are you when using a new banking website without help?
   - low;
   - medium;
   - high.

4. Have you ever compared or applied for a payment/credit card online?
   - yes, in the last 6 months;
   - yes, earlier;
   - no.

5. Have you ever estimated savings interest or a loan repayment online?
   - yes, in the last 6 months;
   - yes, earlier;
   - no.

6. Do you currently use or help administer business banking for a company/organization?
   - yes, including preparing/approving transactions;
   - yes, but not approval workflows;
   - no.

7. Do you have any accessibility or support needs you would like us to accommodate during a remote/web session? Optional free text.

8. Have you worked on, reviewed or been briefed on this Vietbank redesign project?
   - yes → exclude from representative participant sample;
   - no.

## Quota guidance

For 6–8 sessions:
- 4–5 personal-banking participants;
- 2–3 business-banking participants;
- at least 2 mobile-first participants;
- at least 1 participant with low self-reported digital confidence;
- include accessibility/support needs where relevant and feasible.

Do not recruit only coworkers or design/technology professionals and then present results as representative customer evidence.
