VIETBANK WEBSITE — BRAND GUIDELINE + VISUAL SYSTEM REFINEMENT PROMPT

PROJECT:
Redesign/refine the entire Vietbank website into one coherent visual system based on Vietbank's actual brand identity.

Do NOT change approved content architecture unless necessary.
Do NOT redesign Vietbank into a generic fintech startup.

The site must feel like:
TRUSTWORTHY
MODERN
PROFESSIONAL
CLEAR
HUMAN
CONFIDENT
SAFE
DIGITAL-FIRST
PREMIUM BUT ACCESSIBLE

========================================
1. BRAND POSITIONING
========================================

Vietbank is a Vietnamese retail bank with a strong focus on:
- trust
- safety
- sustainable growth
- efficiency
- customer understanding
- compliance
- digital transformation

The digital identity must reflect:
“A modern Vietnamese bank that feels safe, clear, human and increasingly digital.”

Do NOT create:
- crypto aesthetics
- fintech purple gradients
- dark trading-dashboard look
- random glassmorphism
- neon colors
- excessive bento grids
- AI-generated banker imagery
- generic SaaS website style

========================================
2. BRAND COLOR SYSTEM
========================================

Use the colors extracted from the Vietbank logo as the visual foundation.

PRIMARY BLUE:
#0A53A3

Use for:
- primary CTA
- active navigation
- links
- key headings when needed
- icons
- major brand sections
- Digital Plus sections

DEEP BLUE:
#003493

Use for:
- footer
- dark brand sections
- hover state
- strong institutional surfaces
- financial/investor areas

VIETBANK RED:
#D31E25

Use only as a controlled accent.

Use for:
- important notice
- selected promotion
- active emphasis
- alert states
- small brand signature
- campaign details

Do NOT use red as a competing primary color next to blue across the whole interface.

VIETBANK YELLOW:
#FEC414

Secondary warm tone:
#FAAC0C

Use yellow only for:
- highlight
- badge
- illustration details
- decorative geometric elements
- selected campaign accents
- icon background
- small attention cues

Do NOT use white text on yellow.

Use dark navy text on yellow.

SURFACES:

#FFFFFF

#E4EAF0

========================================
3. COLOR USAGE RATIO
========================================

Use approximately:

65–70% White / neutral
20–25% Vietbank Blue family
5–7% Red
3–5% Yellow

Blue must remain visually dominant.

Red and yellow exist to make the brand recognizable, not to compete with blue.

Never create a page where all three brand colors have equal visual weight.

========================================
4. SIGNATURE BRAND COMPOSITION
========================================

Use the geometry of the Vietbank logo as inspiration.

The logo contains:
- strong horizontal wordmark
- blue/red split
- yellow hexagonal / shield-like symbol

Translate these principles into the website without copying the logo shape everywhere.

Possible brand signatures:

1. Small yellow geometric corner accents.
2. Blue/red line or edge transitions.
3. Hexagonal geometry used selectively for:
   - statistic highlights
   - Digital Plus graphics
   - icon containers
   - campaign accents

Do NOT create hexagonal cards everywhere.

Use the hexagonal language as a signature detail, not a layout template.

========================================
5. VISUAL STYLE
========================================

STYLE NAME:
MODERN INSTITUTIONAL BANKING / DIGITAL TRUST

Main characteristics:

- white space
- strong typography
- precise alignment
- restrained brand colors
- structured financial data
- authentic imagery
- clean navigation
- high information clarity

Avoid decorative noise.

Visual hierarchy should come from:
- typography
- spacing
- grid
- scale
- imagery
- brand color

Not from excessive:
- shadows
- borders
- gradients
- floating cards

========================================
6. TYPOGRAPHY
========================================

Primary font:
BE VIETNAM PRO

Use one font family across the entire website.

Reasons:
- excellent Vietnamese diacritics
- high readability
- suitable for financial UI
- modern but not startup-like
- works well for body, heading and numeric data

Weights:

400 Regular
500 Medium
600 SemiBold
700 Bold

Do NOT use unnecessary 300 / 800 / 900 weights.
